# Business Questions

The store sells across 5 categories and 16 states. Sales are growing, but management wants to know if the growth is profitable and where the money is being lost.

| # | Question | Query in `sql/analysis_queries.sql` |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | What are total sales, profit, orders, customers and average order value? | 1 |
| 2 | How did 2025 compare to 2024, and why? | 2, 24, 25, 26 |
| 3 | How do sales and profit move month by month? Which month was best and which was worst? | 3, 4, 5, 6 |
| 4 | Which regions and states bring the most sales and profit? Which are weak? | 7, 8, 9 |
| 5 | Which categories and sub-categories have the best and worst margin? | 10, 11 |
| 6 | Which products sell the most, make the most profit, or lose money? | 12, 13, 14 |
| 7 | Which products sell a lot but make little profit? | 15 |
| 8 | Where do loss-making orders come from? | 16, 21 |
| 9 | Who are the top customers, and how many customers come back? | 17, 18, 19, 20 |
| 10 | How do discounts affect profit? Is the festive sale season profitable? | 21, 22, 23 |
